Hi, I am Akin.
Product Designer focused on clarity and real-world use

I’m a Product Designer working on fintech and SaaS products that need to make complex tasks feel simple and intuitive. Most of my work revolves around financial platforms, business dashboards, and workflows where clarity and usability matter.

I started my career designing interfaces for digital products, gradually moving into more complex systems involving payments, multi currency accounts, and tools used by businesses every day. That shift shaped how I approach design: understanding the problem first, structuring the experience, then refining the visuals.

I care about making products easier to understand and use. Removing friction, simplifying workflows, and helping people complete tasks with confidence, without having to think too much about the interface itself.

Outside of product design, I explore motion graphics, visual storytelling, and creative ideas that let me experiment beyond the product experience.
